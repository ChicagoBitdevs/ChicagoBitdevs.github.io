## BIP54 proved sufficient to thwart Time Warp Attack

Sips showed the two rules introduced by BIP54 are enough. With both in place, there's a hard ceiling on how many blocks a chain can have, given how much time has passed and how much mining work went into it

<https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bounds-on-chain-length-with-bip-54-timewarp-fixes/2899>  
<https://gist.github.com/xstoicunicornx/d73805c930a1c71f5f766a3c6d525b5b>
